Twenty-one stickers, inked in `ink` with palette fills, each pre-tilted inside its own viewBox so it lands at an angle wherever it is placed. Show them with `<img>` at 40, 56, 72 or 96px. No two on one screen at the same size, at most two per screen, never over text.

- Tiny terminal, tiny laptop, tiny keyboard: next to anything about tools and setup.
- Tea mug, tiny plate, ice cream: personal pages and the Saturday dump.
- Complaining cat: the empty state. Cat on keyboard: 404 and a wait that ran long. Golden retriever: a waitlist or an ask.
- Cloud, bubbles, wave, star: background relief on covers and the home top.
- Lumpy heart with the cursor: the sign-off in the footer.
- Arrow curved (violet) and arrow wavy (rose): point at the next thing to do.
- Word stickers (ship it, new, ok but why, it ran, built in a day) in Fredoka 500: they mark state, see the README.
