# Card

Any item in a grid: a resource, a post, a product section, a feature. The consumer provides the moment (know yourself, think, build, check), an eyebrow, a title, one line of text, and optionally a sticker and a word sticker.

- `surface` fill, 1px `line` border, `radius-card`, padding `space-3`. No shadow.
- Three zones top to bottom: `label` eyebrow in the moment's deep tone, the picture zone (sticker or nothing), the words. Cards in a row are equal height and the picture zone absorbs the difference so titles line up.
- The moment dot sits 12px from the top right corner, 10px, in the moment's mid tone. A card with no sticker may use the 3px top bar instead.
- Hover: rise 2px, border to `muted`, `fast`. Focus: the ring. Selected: fill with the moment's soft tone.
- Do: one sticker at most, tilted, sizes 40 to 96. Don't: a left-edge bar, more than one line of description, bold titles.
